# BioTrial Protocol: Investigational New Drug (IND) Safety Reporting Guidelines

## [chunk_biotrial_ind_safety_susar] Expedited SUSAR Reporting Timelines
Sponsors of clinical trials operating under an active Investigational New Drug (IND) application must report any Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reaction (SUSAR) that is fatal or life-threatening to the FDA no later than 7 calendar days after initial receipt of the information. All other non-fatal, unexpected serious adverse drug reactions must be submitted within a mandatory deadline of 15 calendar days via FDA Form 3500A.

## [chunk_biotrial_ind_annual_summary] Annual IND Progress Report Submission Window
The clinical trial sponsor must submit an Annual IND Progress Report to the governing FDA reviewing division within 60 calendar days of the anniversary date that the IND application originally came into effect. The report must detail the total number of subjects enrolled, completed, or withdrawn, summaries of all IND safety reports submitted during the reporting year, and a consolidated log of protocol deviations.

## [chunk_biotrial_ind_clinical_hold] Clinical Hold Remediation and Response Deadlines
When the FDA imposes a full or partial clinical hold halting patient dosing due to an unreasonable and significant risk of illness or injury, the sponsor must submit a comprehensive Complete Response document resolving all cited safety concerns. Following receipt of the sponsor's complete response submission, the regulatory agency has a statutory review deadline of exactly 30 calendar days to lift or maintain the hold.

## [chunk_biotrial_ind_dsmb_charter] Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) Independent Charter
An independent Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) comprising at least 3 voting members (including a board-certified clinical specialist and a doctoral biostatistician) must convene every 6 months to evaluate unblinded interim safety telemetry. Voting members must be completely free of financial or institutional conflicts of interest, and DSMB recommendations for protocol termination require a minimum two-thirds supermajority vote.

## [chunk_biotrial_ind_expanded_access] Expanded Access Compassionate Use Eligibility
Patients with immediately life-threatening illnesses who have exhausted standard approved therapies and do not meet inclusion criteria for ongoing randomized trials may access investigational agents via an Individual Patient Expanded Access IND (Emergency IND). Physicians must obtain written confirmation of Institutional Review Board (IRB) chair concurrence and FDA telephone authorization prior to administering the initial drug dosage.

## [chunk_biotrial_ind_investigator_brochure] Investigator Brochure (IB) Annual Revision Cadence
The clinical sponsor must review and revise the formal Investigator's Brochure (IB) at least once every 12 months. Any significant new preclinical pharmacokinetic findings, metabolic pathways, or human clinical safety signals that alter the established benefit-risk profile must be communicated to active trial investigators via an urgent IB amendment within 30 calendar days.

## [chunk_biotrial_ind_substudy_amendments] Sub-Study and Protocol Biomarker Amendments
Any secondary exploratory sub-study or pharmacogenomic biomarker protocol amendment that requires collecting additional biological specimens exceeding 50 milliliters of whole blood within a 30-day window must receive prior approval from the central IRB before subject screening. Amendments introducing no additional patient risk may be implemented under expedited administrative review.

## [chunk_biotrial_ind_lot_traceability] Investigational Product Lot Tracking and Temperature Excursions
All investigational pharmaceutical product (IPP) packaging units must bear unique serialized 2D datamatrix barcodes tracing manufacturer lot numbers, formulation batch, and expiry dates. If temperature-sensitive biologics experience continuous cold-chain temperature excursions exceeding 8 degrees Celsius for longer than 120 minutes, the clinical site must immediately quarantine the shipment pending sponsor stability clearance.
