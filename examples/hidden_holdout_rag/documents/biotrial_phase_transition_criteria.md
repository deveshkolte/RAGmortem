# BioTrial Protocol: Clinical Trial Phase Transitions and Dose Escalation Rules

## [chunk_biotrial_phase1_dose_limiting_toxicity] Phase I Dose-Limiting Toxicity (DLT) Observation Window
In Phase I first-in-human (FIH) dose-escalation cohorts, the Dose-Limiting Toxicity (DLT) evaluation period spans precisely 28 consecutive days (4 weeks) following administration of Cycle 1 Day 1 study drug. Any drug-related non-hematologic adverse event of Grade 3 or greater (per CTCAE v5.0) or Grade 4 neutropenia lasting longer than 7 consecutive days qualifies as a formal DLT triggering cohort expansion or dose de-escalation.

## [chunk_biotrial_phase1_3plus3_escalation] Classical 3+3 Dose-Escalation Decision Matrix
Dose escalation follows a standard rule-based 3+3 design. If zero of 3 evaluable subjects in a dosing cohort experience a DLT, the trial escalates to the next predefined dose tier. If 1 of 3 subjects experiences a DLT, 3 additional subjects are enrolled at the current dose. If 2 or more subjects among the expanded cohort of 6 experience a DLT, the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) is deemed exceeded, and the preceding dose level is defined as the MTD.

## [chunk_biotrial_phase2_futility_stopping] Phase II Simon Two-Stage Optimum Design and Futility Stoppage
Phase II proof-of-concept efficacy trials utilize Simon's optimal two-stage design with alpha=0.05 and power=80%. In Stage 1, 15 evaluable patients are treated. If fewer than 3 confirmed objective responses (PR or CR under RECIST 1.1) are documented at the primary 12-week radiological restaging assessment, the trial terminates immediately for futility. If 3 or more responses occur, enrollment expands to Stage 2 with an additional 25 subjects.

## [chunk_biotrial_phase3_sample_size_power] Phase III Multi-Center Randomization and Power Calculations
Phase III registrational trials randomize eligible participants in a 1:1 ratio between the investigational arm and standard-of-care comparator stratified by geographical region and prior line of chemotherapy. Sample size modeling assumes a hazard ratio (HR) of 0.70 with 85% statistical power and a two-sided log-rank significance alpha threshold of 0.025, requiring a cumulative total of 320 progression-free survival (PFS) events across all clinical sites.

## [chunk_biotrial_biomarker_companion_diagnostic] Companion Diagnostic (CDx) Validation and Concordance
For molecularly targeted oncology protocols, patient eligibility is restricted to subjects exhibiting confirmed target overexpression verified by an FDA-approved Companion Diagnostic (CDx) immunohistochemistry assay. Clinical analytical concordance between decentralized site testing and the designated centralized reference laboratory must demonstrate an overall percent agreement (OPA) of at least 95.0% across a minimum 100-sample validation cohort.

## [chunk_biotrial_placebo_matching_integrity] Double-Blind Placebo Integrity and Packaging Controls
Placebo control formulations must precisely match the investigational product in color, viscosity, smell, packaging vial dimensions, and tactile consistency to ensure double-blind integrity. Placebo vials contain inert 0.9% sterile saline solution with pharmaceutical-grade caramel coloring. Periodic blind-break audits evaluate blinding efficacy by administering post-treatment questionnaires to treating clinicians and patients.

## [chunk_biotrial_adaptive_midpoint_reestimation] Adaptive Midpoint Sample Size Re-Estimation
The statistical analysis plan permits a single planned sample size re-estimation (SSR) interim review when 50% of targeted primary endpoint events have accrued. If observed conditional power (CP) falls in the promising zone between 36% and 80%, the sponsor may increase total trial sample size by up to a maximum cap of 50% of the baseline cohort without alpha penalty, using the Cui-Hung-Wang preserved test statistic.

## [chunk_biotrial_post_marketing_phase4_commitments] Phase IV Post-Marketing Surveillance and REMS Commitments
Following FDA accelerated approval under 21 CFR 314 Subpart H, the sponsor must initiate a confirmatory randomized Phase IV clinical study within 180 calendar days of commercial market authorization. Failure to achieve protocol milestones or verify anticipated clinical survival benefits within 36 months empowers the FDA to initiate expedited administrative revocation of the marketing license.
