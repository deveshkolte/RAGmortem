# BioTrial Protocol: Informed Consent Administration and Subject Withdrawal Policies

## [chunk_biotrial_consent_version_reconsent] Protocol Amendments and Subject Re-Consent Deadlines
Whenever a protocol amendment alters therapeutic dosing schedules, potential adverse effect profiles, or required investigative visits, active study participants must execute an IRB-approved revised Informed Consent Form (ICF) at or prior to their next scheduled clinical visit. If a participant's next physical visit is scheduled more than 30 calendar days after IRB approval, the site must conduct telephone re-briefing with certified mail delivery of the revised consent.

## [chunk_biotrial_withdrawal_data_retention] Subject Withdrawal of Consent and Existing Data Rights
In accordance with FDA 21 CFR 312.62 and HHS guidance, when a clinical trial participant formally withdraws consent from continued study intervention, the sponsor retains the statutory right to continue analyzing all historical data, laboratory panels, and safety telemetry collected up to the timestamp of withdrawal. Sites are strictly prohibited from deleting or purging previously collected historical records to preserve scientific integrity.

## [chunk_biotrial_withdrawal_biological_samples] Disposition of Archived Biological Biospecimens Upon Withdrawal
Upon formal written request for specimen destruction accompanying withdrawal of consent, clinical sites must destroy all remaining stored blood, plasma, urine, and tumor biopsy specimens within 60 calendar days, provided the specimens have not already been utilized in completed bioanalytical assays. A formal Certificate of Biospecimen Destruction signed by the laboratory director must be placed in the Trial Master File (TMF).

## [chunk_biotrial_lost_to_followup_criteria] Lost to Follow-Up (LTFU) Due Diligence Requirements
A trial subject may only be classified as formally Lost to Follow-Up (LTFU) after the clinical research coordinator documents at least 3 separate telephone contact attempts across different days of the week and sends at least 1 formal certified letter with signature receipt verification to the subject's primary residential address. All communication logs must be archived in the site investigator binder.

## [chunk_biotrial_short_form_consent_interpreters] Non-English Speaking Subjects and Short Form Consent
When enrolling eligible subjects who do not speak or read English and an IRB-approved translated long form consent document is unavailable, study personnel may utilize the Short Form Written Consent process under 21 CFR 50.27(b)(2). The consent discussion requires the physical presence of an independent fluent medical interpreter and an impartial adult witness who signs both the short form and English summary text.

## [chunk_biotrial_lar_decision_hierarchy] Legally Authorized Representative (LAR) Decision Hierarchy
If a prospective clinical study participant lacks decision-making capacity due to acute critical illness or cognitive impairment, informed consent must be solicited from a Legally Authorized Representative (LAR) according to jurisdiction statutory hierarchy: (1) court-appointed legal guardian with healthcare authority, (2) designated durable healthcare power of attorney (POA), (3) lawful spouse, (4) adult child, (5) parent, and (6) adult sibling.

## [chunk_biotrial_compensation_pro_rata_stipend] Subject Travel Reimbursement and Pro-Rata Payment
Subject compensation for travel, parking, and lost wages must be structured on a strict pro-rata basis disbursed per completed study milestone visit, typically $75 to $150 per protocol visit. Completion bonuses or end-of-study lump sums designed to discourage voluntary subject withdrawal are strictly banned by IRB guidelines as unduly coercive under 45 CFR 46.116.

## [chunk_biotrial_emergency_consent_exception_efiec] Exception from Informed Consent (EFIC) for Emergency Research
Under 21 CFR 50.24, investigational interventions for life-threatening acute medical emergencies (e.g. traumatic brain injury, out-of-hospital refractory cardiac arrest) where subjects cannot provide consent and no LAR is available within the therapeutic window require Exception from Informed Consent (EFIC). EFIC protocols mandate prior public community consultation and local public disclosure meetings before enrollment begins.
