# BioTrial Protocol: Institutional Review Board (IRB) Oversight and Adverse Event Triage

## [chunk_biotrial_irb_prompt_reporting] Serious Adverse Event (SAE) Site-to-Sponsor Reporting
Principal Investigators at participating clinical study sites must notify the clinical sponsor's pharmacovigilance safety officer of any Serious Adverse Event (SAE) within 24 hours of first awareness, regardless of judged causality. Written documentation detailing hospitalization records, diagnostic laboratory panels, and concomitant medications must be transmitted through the electronic data capture (EDC) system within 48 hours.

## [chunk_biotrial_irb_continuing_review] IRB Continuing Review and Re-Approval Timelines
All active interventional clinical trials must undergo formal Institutional Review Board (IRB) continuing review at intervals not exceeding 365 calendar days (1 year). Clinical sites must submit complete renewal applications, current subject accrual tables, and cumulative safety event registries at least 45 calendar days prior to protocol expiration to prevent lapse of ethical authorization.

## [chunk_biotrial_irb_unanticipated_problems] Unanticipated Problems Involving Risks to Subjects (UPIRSO)
Any event or incident meeting the triad criteria of being unexpected in nature, related or possibly related to trial participation, and suggesting that subjects or others are placed at greater risk of harm than previously recognized must be reported to the convened IRB within 10 business days of determination. Systemic dosage adjustments must be suspended across the site until IRB review.

## [chunk_biotrial_irb_composition_quorum] Institutional Review Board Quorum and Membership Standards
IRB committees reviewing FDA-regulated Phase I-III drug trials must maintain a roster of at least 5 voting members representing diverse backgrounds, including at least one scientific member (e.g. physician, toxicologist), at least one non-scientific member (e.g. bioethicist, clergy), and at least one unaffiliated community member. Quorum requires a simple majority with at least one non-scientist present.

## [chunk_biotrial_irb_vulnerable_populations] Vulnerable Subject Populations and Additional Protections
Research protocols enrolling vulnerable populations, including pediatric patients (under age 18), cognitively impaired individuals, or economically disadvantaged subjects, require mandatory supplemental safeguards under 45 CFR 46 Subparts B-D. Pediatric enrollment mandates assent procedures for children age 7 and older alongside double parental permission unless one parent is deceased or legally incompetent.

## [chunk_biotrial_irb_protocol_deviation] Protocol Deviations and Serious Non-Compliance Notifications
Major protocol deviations that directly compromise subject safety or data integrity—such as administering investigational products exceeding maximum tolerated dose or enrolling subjects failing vital cardiac eligibility criteria—must be reported to the IRB within 5 calendar days. Minor administrative deviations (e.g. a routine follow-up visit scheduled 2 days out of window) are documented in quarterly review logs.

## [chunk_biotrial_irb_emergency_unblinding] Emergency Code Break and Patient Unblinding Procedures
Emergency code-break envelopes or automated electronic unblinding tools may be activated by the treating investigator only when immediate knowledge of the assigned study intervention (active therapeutic candidate versus matched placebo) is strictly necessary to direct life-saving medical management. The investigator must document the medical rationale within 2 hours and notify the sponsor medical monitor within 12 hours.

## [chunk_biotrial_irb_financial_disclosure] Clinical Investigator Financial Conflict of Interest Disclosures
All clinical sub-investigators and study coordinators involved in patient screening and data collection must execute FDA Form 1572 and submit financial disclosure statements certifying equity holdings, consulting royalties, and proprietary patent interests exceeding $50,000 in the clinical trial sponsor. Updated financial declarations are required annually and for 1 year following trial completion.
