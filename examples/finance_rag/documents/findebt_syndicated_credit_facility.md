# Syndicated Senior Secured Credit Agreement — Pricing and Payment Mechanics

## [chunk_findebt_sofr_interest_margin] Term Benchmark SOFR Margin and Tranche Structure
Borrowings under the Initial Term B Facility shall bear interest at an annual rate equal to Term SOFR for the applicable Interest Period plus an applicable margin of 3.25% per annum for Term SOFR loans, or Base Rate plus 2.25% per annum for Base Rate loans. If the Consolidated First Lien Net Leverage Ratio is less than or equal to 3.50 to 1.00, the applicable margin shall step down by 0.25% per annum to 3.00% for Term SOFR borrowings. The Term SOFR floor is established at 0.50% per annum across all interest calculation cycles.

## [chunk_findebt_mandatory_excess_cash_flow] Mandatory Prepayments from Annual Excess Cash Flow
Within 5 business days following the delivery of annual audited financial statements under Section 9.1(a), the Borrower shall prepay outstanding Term Loans in an aggregate principal amount equal to 50% of Excess Cash Flow for such fiscal year; provided that such percentage shall be reduced to 25% if the Consolidated First Lien Net Leverage Ratio is less than or equal to 3.75 to 1.00, and 0% if the Consolidated First Lien Net Leverage Ratio is less than or equal to 3.25 to 1.00. Prepayments shall be applied pro rata across remaining amortization payments.

## [chunk_findebt_commitment_fee_undrawn_facility] Revolving Credit Facility Undrawn Commitment Fee
The Borrower agrees to pay to the Administrative Agent, for the account of each Revolving Credit Lender, an undrawn commitment fee equal to 0.375% per annum on the average daily unutilized portion of the Revolving Credit Commitments. The commitment fee shall accrue from the Closing Date and shall be payable in arrears on the last business day of each calendar quarter (March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31) and on the Revolving Credit Termination Date.

## [chunk_findebt_amortization_schedule] Quarterly Term Loan Amortization Schedule
The Borrower shall repay the principal of the Initial Term Loans in consecutive quarterly installments on the last business day of each March, June, September, and December, commencing with the first full fiscal quarter following the Closing Date. Each quarterly installment shall equal 0.25% of the original aggregate principal amount of the Initial Term Loans ($2,500,000 per quarter), with the remaining unpaid principal balance payable in full on the Term Loan Maturity Date.

## [chunk_findebt_voluntary_prepayment_call_protection] Voluntary Prepayments and Soft Call Protection
The Borrower may prepay the Term Loans in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty, subject to Section 3.5 breakage costs; provided that if a Repricing Event occurs within six (6) months of the Closing Date, such prepayment shall be accompanied by a premium equal to 1.00% of the aggregate principal amount of the Term Loans prepaid or repriced. After the expiration of the six-month period, voluntary prepayments may be executed at par.

## [chunk_findebt_asset_sale_sweep] Net Cash Proceeds Asset Disposition Sweep
If the Borrower or any Restricted Subsidiary disposes of property or assets outside the ordinary course of business generating Net Cash Proceeds exceeding $10,000,000 in any fiscal year, the Borrower shall prepay the Term Loans by an amount equal to 100% of such excess proceeds within 10 business days; provided that the Borrower may reinvest such Net Cash Proceeds in productive long-term assets within 365 calendar days of receipt.

## [chunk_findebt_default_interest_spread] Default Interest Rate Surcharge
Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default under Section 11.1(a) (Failure to Pay Principal or Interest), all outstanding principal, overdue interest, and fees shall bear interest at an additional default rate equal to 2.00% per annum above the rate otherwise applicable until such overdue amount is paid in full or the underlying Event of Default is waived in writing by Required Lenders.

## [chunk_findebt_term_loan_maturity_date] Facility Final Maturity Dates
The Initial Term B Loans shall mature on the seventh (7th) anniversary of the Closing Date (the 'Term Loan Maturity Date'). The Revolving Credit Facility commitments shall terminate and all outstanding revolving advances shall mature on the fifth (5th) anniversary of the Closing Date. No extension of maturity shall be effective without the unanimous consent of each directly affected Lender.
