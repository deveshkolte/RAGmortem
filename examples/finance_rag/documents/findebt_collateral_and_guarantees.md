# Collateral, Guarantees, Perfection, and Security Documents

## [chunk_findebt_first_priority_lien_scope] Scope of Perfected Collateral Liens
All Obligations of the Borrower are secured by a first-priority perfected lien on substantially all tangible and intangible assets of the Borrower and Subsidiary Guarantors, including accounts receivable, inventory, intellectual property, equipment, deposit accounts, and 100% of the equity interests in all domestic Restricted Subsidiaries and 65% of the voting equity in first-tier foreign subsidiaries.

## [chunk_findebt_subsidiary_guarantor_joinder] Joinder Requirements for Newly Formed Subsidiaries
Within thirty (30) calendar days of acquiring or forming any new domestic Restricted Subsidiary (or 45 calendar days in the case of foreign entities where local pledge is required), the Borrower shall cause such subsidiary to execute a Subsidiary Guaranty, Security Agreement Joinder, and deliver legal enforceability opinions to the Collateral Agent.

## [chunk_findebt_deposit_account_control_agreements] Deposit Account Control Agreement (DACA) Timing
The Borrower and each Subsidiary Guarantor must deliver fully executed Deposit Account Control Agreements (DACAs) in favor of the Collateral Agent for every material deposit, securities, or commodity account within sixty (60) calendar days of the Closing Date; excluding payroll, trust, tax withholding, and petty cash accounts with balances below $2,500,000.

## [chunk_findebt_intellectual_property_security_recording] USPTO and Copyright Office Security Filings
Security interests in registered patents, trademarks, and copyrights must be perfected by filing short-form Grant of Security Interest documents with the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) and United States Copyright Office within ninety (90) calendar days of the Closing Date or subsequent acquisition of such IP assets.

## [chunk_findebt_real_property_mortgage_criteria] Material Real Estate Mortgage Threshold
Mortgages on fee-owned real property shall only be required if the fair market value of an individual property exceeds $15,000,000 on the Closing Date or acquisition date. Leased real property and leasehold interests are excluded from collateral requirements.

## [chunk_findebt_collateral_release_permitted_disposition] Automatic Collateral Release Upon Permitted Asset Sale
Upon any permitted sale, transfer, or disposition of collateral to an unaffiliated third party in accordance with Section 10.5, the liens granted to the Collateral Agent on such disposed asset shall automatically terminate and be released without further action by Lenders upon confirmation of receipt of Net Cash Proceeds.

## [chunk_findebt_intercreditor_subordinated_debt] Subordination and Intercreditor Agreement Terms
Any junior-lien or subordinated indebtedness permitted under Section 10.1 must be subject to an Intercreditor Agreement providing for a customary 180-day standstill period on junior lien enforcement following notice of a senior Event of Default and standard turnover provisions of proceeds received in bankruptcy.

## [chunk_findebt_insurance_loss_payee_endorsements] Property Insurance and Lender Loss Payee Clauses
The Borrower shall maintain commercial property, business interruption, and general liability insurance policies naming the Collateral Agent as an additional insured and sole loss payee. Insurers must provide thirty (30) calendar days' prior written notice to the Collateral Agent before canceling or materially reducing policy coverage.
