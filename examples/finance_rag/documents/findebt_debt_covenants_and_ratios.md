# Financial Covenants, Leverage Ratios, and Negative Covenants

## [chunk_findebt_max_first_lien_leverage_ratio] Maximum First Lien Net Leverage Ratio
The Borrower shall not permit the Consolidated First Lien Net Leverage Ratio, determined as of the last day of any fiscal quarter ending on or after September 30, to exceed 4.25 to 1.00. This financial covenant shall be tested on a springing basis only if the aggregate outstanding balance of Revolving Loans and Letters of Credit exceeds 35% of the total Revolving Credit Commitments on such test date.

## [chunk_findebt_interest_coverage_ratio] Minimum Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio
The Borrower shall maintain a Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio of not less than 3.00 to 1.00 for each trailing twelve-month period ending on the last day of each fiscal quarter. 'Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio' is defined as Consolidated EBITDA divided by Consolidated Cash Interest Expense for the applicable measurement period.

## [chunk_findebt_restricted_payments_basket] Restricted Payments General Basket and Dividend Conditions
Neither the Borrower nor any Restricted Subsidiary shall declare or pay any dividend or distribution, or purchase any equity interests, unless: (a) no Event of Default exists, (b) the Consolidated Total Net Leverage Ratio is less than 3.50 to 1.00 on a pro forma basis, and (c) the payment is funded under the Cumulative Available Amount or the general non-springing basket capped at $25,000,000 per fiscal year.

## [chunk_findebt_debt_incurrence_basket] Permitted Indebtedness Ratios and General Debt Baskets
The Borrower shall not incur additional Indebtedness except: (a) Pari passu incremental term facilities not exceeding the Shared Incremental Amount of $150,000,000 plus an unlimited amount subject to meeting a Consolidated First Lien Net Leverage Ratio of 4.25 to 1.00; (b) Capital lease and purchase money obligations capped at $30,000,000; and (c) General unsecured indebtedness capped at $50,000,000.

## [chunk_findebt_annual_capital_expenditure_limit] Maximum Annual Capital Expenditures Limit
Consolidated Capital Expenditures of the Borrower and its Restricted Subsidiaries shall not exceed $75,000,000 in any single fiscal year; provided that 50% of any unutilized capital expenditure allowance from the immediately preceding fiscal year may be carried forward into the subsequent fiscal year (with carried-forward amounts treated as utilized first).

## [chunk_findebt_permitted_acquisitions_criteria] Permitted Acquisition Thresholds and Conditions
The Borrower may consummate acquisitions of entities or operating assets provided that: (1) the acquired target is in an identical or complementary line of business, (2) pro forma Consolidated Total Net Leverage does not exceed 4.50 to 1.00, (3) minimum Liquidity of $40,000,000 is maintained immediately following closing, and (4) aggregate consideration for non-guarantor subsidiaries does not exceed $25,000,000.

## [chunk_findebt_disposition_negative_covenant] Negative Pledge on Material Asset Transfers
The Borrower shall not convey, sell, lease, or transfer all or substantially all of its properties or assets to any third party in a single transaction or series of related transactions without the prior written consent of 100% of Lenders, except for intercompany mergers among Loan Parties where the surviving entity is a Loan Party.

## [chunk_findebt_affiliate_transactions_threshold] Arms-Length Affiliate Transaction Threshold
The Borrower shall not enter into any commercial transaction with any Affiliate involving payments or consideration exceeding $5,000,000 unless: (a) terms are substantially as favorable as would be obtained in a comparable arm's-length transaction with an unaffiliated third party, and (b) transactions exceeding $15,000,000 receive prior approval from a majority of disinterested board directors.
