# Affirmative Reporting Covenants, SEC Disclosures, and Audit Timing

## [chunk_findebt_annual_audited_financials_deadline] Annual Audited Financial Statements Delivery Deadline
Within ninety (90) calendar days after the end of each fiscal year (or such earlier deadline as mandated by the SEC for Form 10-K filings), the Borrower shall deliver consolidated balance sheets and statements of operations audited by a nationally recognized independent registered accounting firm ('Big Four' or other firm approved by Required Lenders), without a 'going concern' explanatory qualification.

## [chunk_findebt_quarterly_unaudited_financials_deadline] Quarterly Financial Report Delivery Deadline
Within forty-five (45) calendar days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters of each fiscal year (or such earlier deadline mandated for Form 10-Q filings), the Borrower shall deliver unaudited consolidated balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements, certified by the Chief Financial Officer as fairly presenting financial position in accordance with GAAP.

## [chunk_findebt_quarterly_compliance_certificate] CFO Compliance Certificate Delivery and Ratio Calculations
Concurrently with the delivery of annual and quarterly financial statements, the Borrower shall deliver a Compliance Certificate signed by the Chief Financial Officer or Treasurer setting forth in reasonable detail the calculations required to establish compliance with Section 10.1 (Leverage Ratio) and Section 10.2 (Interest Coverage Ratio).

## [chunk_findebt_annual_operating_budget_forecast] Annual Operating Budget and Financial Forecast Delivery
Within thirty (30) calendar days after the commencement of each fiscal year, the Borrower shall deliver to the Administrative Agent an annual operating budget and projected financial model on a month-by-month basis, including projected EBITDA, capital expenditures, and free cash flows for such upcoming fiscal year.

## [chunk_findebt_lender_quarterly_earnings_call] Quarterly Lender Conference Call Protocol
Within five (5) business days following the delivery of quarterly or annual financial statements, the Borrower's executive leadership team (including the Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer) shall host a live telephonic conference call with all Lenders to review operational performance and answer credit questions.

## [chunk_findebt_notice_of_litigation_threshold] Mandatory Notice of Material Pending Litigation
The Borrower shall promptly, and in any event within five (5) business days after learning thereof, notify the Administrative Agent in writing of any litigation, arbitration, or governmental investigation initiated against any Loan Party involving potential liability exceeding $15,000,000.

## [chunk_findebt_environmental_remediation_notice] Environmental Claims and Hazardous Discharge Reporting
The Borrower shall notify the Administrative Agent within ten (10) business days of receiving notice of any CERCLA or federal environmental cleanup citation, hazardous material discharge, or toxic tort claim where the potential remediation cost exceeds $10,000,000.

## [chunk_findebt_patriot_act_kyc_compliance] Beneficial Ownership and USA PATRIOT Act KYC Verification
Each Lender subject to the USA PATRIOT Act and FinCEN Customer Due Diligence Requirements hereby notifies the Borrower that it requires verification of identity and beneficial ownership certifications prior to funding any Loan advance or assignment transfer.
