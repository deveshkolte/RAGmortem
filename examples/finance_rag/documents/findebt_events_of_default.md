# Events of Default, Grace Periods, and Acceleration Remedies

## [chunk_findebt_payment_default_grace_period] Payment Defaults and Applicable Grace Periods
It shall constitute an Event of Default under Section 11.1 if the Borrower fails to pay: (1) any principal installment of any Loan when due (with zero grace period), or (2) any interest, commitment fee, or administrative fee within five (5) business days after the scheduled due date thereof.

## [chunk_findebt_covenant_breach_cure_period] Negative and Affirmative Covenant Cure Windows
Failure to comply with any negative covenant under Article X constitutes an immediate Event of Default without any cure period. Failure to observe any affirmative covenant under Article IX constitutes an Event of Default only if such failure continues unremedied for thirty (30) calendar days after written notice from the Administrative Agent or Required Lenders.

## [chunk_findebt_cross_default_dollar_threshold] Cross-Default and Cross-Acceleration Threshold
An Event of Default shall occur if the Borrower or any Material Subsidiary fails to pay any principal or interest on any Indebtedness exceeding an aggregate principal amount of $50,000,000 beyond the applicable grace period, or if any event occurs that permits the holders of such debt to declare it immediately due and payable prior to scheduled maturity.

## [chunk_findebt_bankruptcy_automatic_acceleration] Involuntary and Voluntary Bankruptcy Acceleration
Upon the filing of a voluntary bankruptcy petition under Title 11 of the United States Code by any Loan Party, all outstanding Term Loans, Revolving Loans, accrued interest, and fees shall automatically become immediately due and payable without presentment, demand, protest, or notice of any kind. Involuntary filings trigger default if not dismissed within sixty (60) calendar days.

## [chunk_findebt_erisa_funding_deficiency_default] ERISA Termination and Funding Deficiency Default
An Event of Default occurs if the Borrower or any ERISA Affiliate incurs unfunded pension liabilities or PBGC statutory liens under Section 4068 of ERISA in an aggregate amount exceeding $25,000,000, or if a pension plan termination proceeding is instituted by the Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation.

## [chunk_findebt_material_judgment_default_threshold] Unsatisfied Final Monetary Judgment Threshold
An Event of Default occurs if one or more final judgments or orders for the payment of money exceeding $35,000,000 (net of undisputed insurance coverage) are rendered against the Borrower or any Restricted Subsidiary, and such judgments remain unstayed, unsatisfied, or unbonded for forty-five (45) consecutive calendar days.

## [chunk_findebt_change_of_control_default] Change of Control Definition and Mandatory Offer
The occurrence of a Change of Control constitutes an immediate Event of Default. A 'Change of Control' occurs if: (a) any person or syndicate acquires beneficial ownership of more than 35% of the total voting power of the Borrower's capital stock, or (b) a majority of board seats are occupied by individuals not nominated or endorsed by the incumbent board.

## [chunk_findebt_lender_acceleration_remedies] Required Lenders Acceleration Vote and Voting Threshold
Upon the occurrence of any uncured Event of Default (other than automatic bankruptcy acceleration), the Administrative Agent, upon the written direction of the Required Lenders (holding more than 50.0% of the aggregate outstanding Loans and Commitments), may terminate all commitments, declare all obligations due and payable, and instruct the Collateral Agent to enforce remedies against collateral.
